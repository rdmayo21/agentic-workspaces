### 2027-02-18 — Príncipe Real instead of Alfama

**Decision:** Rent the "Jardim flat" in Príncipe Real, not the Alfama
terrace apartment. Supersedes 2027-02-03.
**Rationale:** Two things we didn't know on Feb 3. (1) The Alfama listing
is a fourth-floor walk-up with no lift — 84 steps per the host's reply on
2027-02-16 — and Sam is six weeks out from knee surgery. (2) Three recent
reviews mention tram noise from 06:30. Príncipe Real is flat, has a lift,
is €30 more per night, and is a 12-minute walk to the same Baixa metro
stops. The terrace was nice; the stairs would have decided every evening
for us. Shortlist updated in `references/apartment-shortlist-2027-02.md`.
**Outcome:** decided, not yet booked; free-cancellation rate holds until
2027-03-10 (STATUS.md).

### 2027-03-02 — Sintra by train, no rental car

**Decision:** Day trip to Sintra on Tue May 11 by train from Rossio; no
car for the trip.
**Rationale:** Parking in Sintra on a weekday in May is reported as full by

minutes and ~€5 each way; and a car we only use once costs ~€60/day plus
the garage in Lisbon. Tuesday because Pena Palace is quieter than
weekends and Monday is a common closure day for other sights.
**Outcome:** decided; timed tickets to buy once the date is inside the
sales window (NEXT-ACTIONS.md).
