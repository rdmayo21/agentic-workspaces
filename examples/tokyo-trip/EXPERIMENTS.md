# Experiments

Pre-registered tests against reality, append-only. The ledger's value is the
time order: criteria written BEFORE the result exists cannot be bent to fit
it.

### 2027-01-08 — Award redemption vs. paying cash

**Hypothesis:** With the Apr 3–15 window locked (DECISIONS.md 2027-01-08),
award space on a SFO⇄Tokyo nonstop can still be found for bloom-season
dates, saving most of the ~$1,150 cash fare.
**Method:** Search UA, ANA, and JAL award availability for the Apr 3
outbound and Apr 15 return, once each morning on Jan 8 and Jan 9.
**Criteria (pre-registered):** Economy or premium-economy award on a
nonstop by end of Jan 9 = book it, cancel the cash-fare watch. Nothing by
then = award space for peak-bloom dates doesn't survive to ~T-90 on this
route — book the cash fare by Jan 10, before spring pricing moves, and stop
monitoring.
**Result:** RESOLVED 2027-01-10 — null. No award space on any nonstop for
either date across all three programs. Booked UA 837/838 cash at $1,140 on
2027-01-10 (BOOKINGS.md).
**What it changed:** Flights moved to CONFIRMED (STATUS.md). The null is
evidence that bloom-season award space is gone by January — for a future
spring trip, run this search the prior autumn instead. Note the null was
pre-declared as *book cash now*, not as "keep waiting" — the exit date was
set before the first search.
